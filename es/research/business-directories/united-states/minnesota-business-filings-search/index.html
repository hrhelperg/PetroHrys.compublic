Search Business Filings

Search Business Filings is the state business-entity register operated by the Office of the Minnesota Secretary of State. Search by Business Name or File Number, with three refinements exposed directly on the search form: Search Scope (Begins With / Contains), Filing Status (Active / Inactive) and Include Prior Names (Exclude / Include). Whether filed document images can be viewed free of charge is not stated on the official pages.

Assessment

Strengths

  • Search Business Filings can be searched free of charge and without an account, directly from the Office of the Minnesota Secretary of State.
  • The Office of the Minnesota Secretary of State is the statutory source for Minnesota, so an entry exists because a filing was made — not because anyone submitted a listing.

Limitations

  • No official page states whether filed document images can be viewed free of charge, so the cost of reading a filing cannot be established in advance.
  • It does not prove good standing, that an annual renewal has been filed (Minnesota entities can lapse for non-renewal), that any professional or occupational licence is held, that taxes are paid, that the business trades, or anything about ownership, solvency or creditworthiness.

Records are created through incorporation, registration, filing, or statutory processes.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
