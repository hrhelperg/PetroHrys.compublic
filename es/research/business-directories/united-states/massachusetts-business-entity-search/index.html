Valoración

Puntos fuertes

  • Search for a Business Entity can be searched free of charge and without an account, directly from the Secretary of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts — Corporations Division.
  • The Secretary of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts — Corporations Division is the statutory source for Massachusetts, so an entry exists because a filing was made — not because anyone submitted a listing.

Limitaciones

  • No official page states whether filed document images can be viewed free of charge, so the cost of reading a filing cannot be established in advance.
  • Part of Search for a Business Entity sits behind payment, an account or another restriction, so the free tier answers only part of a due-diligence question.
  • It does not prove good standing — Massachusetts sells Certificates of Good Standing separately — nor licensing (occupational and professional licences are wholly outside this division), nor tax registration or compliance with the Department of Revenue, nor solvency or creditworthiness.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
