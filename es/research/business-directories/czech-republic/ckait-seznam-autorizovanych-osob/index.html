Selections from the list of authorised engineers and technicians

The Czech Chamber of Chartered Engineers and Technicians in Construction publishes selections from its list of authorised engineers and technicians. Authorisation is required to carry out selected activities in construction reserved to authorised persons.

Valoración

Puntos fuertes

  • Authorisation is required for reserved construction activities, so an entry verifies an entitlement to act.
  • Engineers and technicians are distinguished within one chamber’s list, which reflects how the authorisations actually differ.
  • It is kept by the chamber that grants the authorisation, making it the authoritative Czech source.

Limitaciones

  • The page presents selections from the list rather than announcing itself as the complete register, so completeness is not claimed here.
  • Its form requires JavaScript, so the search could not be exercised and nothing about result content or coverage is asserted.
  • It covers engineers and technicians in construction only. Architects are elsewhere, and absence here says nothing about them.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
