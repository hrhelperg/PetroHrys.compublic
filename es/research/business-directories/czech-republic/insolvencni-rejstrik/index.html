Insolvency Register

Insolvenční rejstřík is the Czech public insolvency register kept under the Insolvency Act, publishing insolvency proceedings, the decisions issued in them and the associated case files. Publication in the register is how insolvency decisions are made publicly effective in the Czech Republic.

Valoración

Puntos fuertes

  • The register is free to search and needs no account.
  • Publication in the register is the statutory means by which Czech insolvency decisions take public effect, so the register is part of the procedure rather than a report on it.
  • The case file associated with a proceeding is published alongside the decisions, so the underlying documents can be read.

Limitaciones

  • It records insolvency proceedings only. It establishes nothing about whether a company exists, who represents it or whether it is otherwise in good standing.
  • Entries are removed once the statutory retention period expires, so an absent result does not prove that a party was never insolvent.
  • The register concerns a legal procedure and is not a general assessment of creditworthiness.

Ideal para

  • Checking whether a Czech person or company is subject to insolvency proceedings and reading the decisions issued in them.

No recomendado para

  • Confirming that a company exists or who represents it, which is the public register kept by the courts.
  • Treating an empty result as proof that a party has never been insolvent, because entries are removed after the statutory period.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
