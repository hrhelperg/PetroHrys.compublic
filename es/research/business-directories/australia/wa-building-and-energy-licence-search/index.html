WA Online Licence Search

WA Online Licence Search is the state register operated by the Department of Local Government, Industry Regulation and Safety. It records holders of occupational licences, registrations, permits, certificates and accreditations issued under Western Australian law by three divisions of the department. Search fields: A licence type must be selected first; the form then exposes, per type, company/business or family name, first name, location or post code, a search-all-WA option, date of birth (for individual credentials such as High Risk Work Licence and Construction Induction Training Card), and charity category for the Charity type. The application also carries a licence-number lookup path. Records returned include the licensee or entity name, licence number, licence type and sub-type (individual, body corporate/company, partnership), licence status, expiry date, entity type, trading names where held, and a business locality.

Visit WA Online Licence Search (opens in a new tab)ols.demirs.wa.gov.au

Valoración

Puntos fuertes

  • WA Online Licence Search can be searched free of charge and without an account, directly from the Department of Local Government, Industry Regulation and Safety.
  • For construction and workplace-safety due diligence it also confirms high risk work, asbestos removal and demolition authorisations.
  • Covers 35 distinct licence types spanning three separate regulators in one department — Building and Energy, Consumer Protection and WorkSafe — so it is a department-wide occupational licensing search, not a building-industry tool.
  • ASIC registers companies, business names, financial advisers and banned persons under federal law. This register records Western Australian occupational authorisations that ASIC does not hold at all: electrical contractors and workers, gasfitting permits, plumbing contractors, tradesperson plumbers and restricted plumbing permits, building and painting contractors and practitioners, building surveying contractors and practitioners, motor vehicle dealers, salespersons, repairers, repair businesses and yard managers, real estate and settlement agents, land valuers, debt collectors, employment agents, charitable collections licences, and WorkSafe high risk work licences and assessor accreditations, asbestos assessor and Class A/B asbestos removal licences, demolition licences and construction induction training cards.

Limitaciones

  • It does not establish corporate existence, legal personality or entity identifiers.
  • Contact details are withheld by law — the department's own form states "email and telephone details cannot be released under the relevant legislation."
  • Downloadable bulk registers exist only for builders, building surveyors, painters and building engineers; the other 31 licence types are query-only.
  • The free tier returns index data; extracts, certificates and lodged documents are a separate paid product.
