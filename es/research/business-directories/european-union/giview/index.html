GIview

GIview is the search portal for geographical indications protected in the European Union. Responsibility for the underlying registers is split: the European Commission registers geographical indications for agricultural products, wines and spirit drinks, while the EU Intellectual Property Office is responsible for those covering craft and industrial products.

Valoración

Puntos fuertes

  • It brings EU geographical indications into one search rather than requiring a reader to know which authority protects which category.
  • It is operated within the official EU intellectual property network rather than by a commercial aggregator.
  • It is scoped to geographical indications, which keeps a distinct right from being folded into trade mark tooling.

Limitaciones

  • It is a search portal over registers whose responsibility is split between the Commission and the intellectual property office; it is not itself the constitutive register for either.
  • The portal is a client-rendered application that could not be exercised, so its search behaviour and coverage were not observed.
  • A geographical indication is not a trade mark. An entry says nothing about whether any brand using the name is registrable or lawful.

Ideal para

  • Checking whether a product name is protected in the EU as a geographical indication or designation of origin.
  • Finding which EU protection applies to a food, wine, spirit or craft and industrial product name.

No recomendado para

  • Trade mark searching. A geographical indication protects origin, not a trader’s brand, and the two are separate rights on separate registers.
  • Assuming one authority is behind everything shown, because responsibility for EU geographical indications is split by product category.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
