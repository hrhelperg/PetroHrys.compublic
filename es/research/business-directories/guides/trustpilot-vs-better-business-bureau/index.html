Trustpilot vs Better Business Bureau

A field-by-field comparison of Trustpilot and Better Business Bureau using only verified data, including where the evidence is insufficient.

Introduction

Both carry consumer feedback, but they are structurally different institutions: one is an open review platform, the other a membership organisation with a paid accreditation programme and a complaints process.

Advantages: Trustpilot

  • Free account tier confirmed on the business site
  • Widely recognised consumer trust signal

Advantages: Better Business Bureau

  • Long-established complaint record
  • Covers the United States and Canada

Disadvantages: Trustpilot

  • Open review model is susceptible to review manipulation
  • Advanced features are paid

Disadvantages: Better Business Bureau

  • Accreditation is a paid programme, which draws recurring criticism of the rating model

Which to choose

Neither is a substitute for the other. Read the table above against your own situation: the fields that differ are the ones that should decide it, and the fields marked Unknown are questions to put to the directory rather than assumptions to make.

Where the two scores differ, the gap reflects the ten editorial factors documented in the scoring methodology, not popularity or traffic.

Frequently asked questions

Is one of these better than the other?

They are not interchangeable, so the question is which fits a given situation. This comparison sets out the verified differences rather than declaring a winner.

Why are some fields Unknown?

Because they were not stated on the official sources that were verified. They are shown as Unknown rather than estimated.
