If You Are a Freelancer

What the verified dataset can and cannot tell an independent professional about directory listings.

Introduction

This is the thinnest area of the dataset, and saying so is more useful than padding the page. Only a small number of verified directories record that they accept freelancers, and most of those are agency directories where an independent professional competes against firms.

The honest recommendation is therefore narrow.

Evidence base

Evidence base: 63 of 295 verified directories in the PetroHrys dataset accept freelancers. Every figure and table on this page is generated from those records at build time. Fields that were never established are shown as Unknown rather than filled in.

This is a thin area of the dataset. The recommendation below is deliberately narrow rather than padded, and absence here means unrecorded rather than unavailable.

Decision framework

  • Treat agency directories as a secondary channel rather than a primary one.
  • Prioritise the platform marketplaces relevant to your craft, where the artefact rather than the firm is listed.
  • Confirm any statutory registration that applies to you as a sole trader.

Advantages recorded across these directories

  • It is the official nationwide directory and covers every lawyer admitted in Germany, so one search replaces twenty-eight regional ones.
  • It reaches beyond German lawyers to European lawyers and to lawyers from other states established in Germany under section 206 of the Federal Lawyers Act.
  • It is served in both German and English, which is unusual for a national professional register.
  • It is kept by the state audit regulator itself rather than by a professional association.
  • It covers individual auditors, audit firms and auditors and firms from third countries in one register.
  • Sanctions entered in the register are published, which is unusual and directly useful when checking standing.

Disadvantages recorded across these directories

  • Admission is required to practise, not only to use the title, so an entry means more here than in a protected-title register — but it still says nothing about competence in any field.
  • The federal chamber operates the directory; it is not the body that admitted anyone. Admission and discipline sit with the regional bar, which the search page states directly.
  • It records individual lawyers and their firms, not a firm’s quality, and specialist subtitles are conferred by the local bar rather than centrally.
  • The chamber states it is not entitled to give legal advice, to examine the conduct of individual lawyers, to recommend them, or to check fee invoices, so inclusion establishes none of those things.
  • No search was executed during verification, so result content and any limits were not observed and none is asserted.
  • Registration is required to carry out statutory audits, so this is a licence register — but it says nothing about the quality of any audit.

Recommendations

Work through the framework above in order and stop at the point where the next listing has no specific purpose.

When not to use this

  • Freelancers expecting directory listings to replace direct referral. The dataset provides no evidence they do.

Frequently asked questions

Why so few options?

Because only directories that met the verification standard are included, and freelancer acceptance is rarely stated explicitly on official pages. Absence here means unrecorded, not unavailable.
