National Court Register

Krajowy Rejestr Sądowy is the Polish National Court Register, kept by the registry courts under the Act of 20 August 1997. It comprises the register of entrepreneurs — commercial companies, cooperatives, state enterprises and branches of foreign entrepreneurs — together with the register of associations, other social and professional organisations, foundations and public healthcare institutions.

Valoración

Puntos fuertes

  • It is the constitutive register for Polish companies, cooperatives, associations and foundations, so an entry establishes legal existence rather than merely describing a business.
  • The Ministry of Justice publishes a free open API that returns a complete current extract for an entity without any account, which makes verification reproducible rather than screen-dependent.
  • One register covers both the commercial sector and the association and foundation sector, through two separate statutory sub-registers.
  • Financial documents filed by registered entities are held in the associated document repository, so filings can be reached from the register entry.

Limitaciones

  • It does not cover sole traders. A Polish natural person trading on their own account is registered in CEIDG, and neither register contains the other’s population.
  • The NIP and REGON numbers shown in a KRS extract originate in the tax register and in the REGON statistical register and are placed in KRS automatically; they are not established by the registry court.
  • The public web search sits behind bot protection that refused automated requests, so its interactive behaviour, any fee for certified output and any CAPTCHA could not be observed.
  • An entry records legal existence and filed data. It is not a statement about solvency, creditworthiness or the conduct of the entity.

No recomendado para

  • Treating an entry as evidence of solvency, creditworthiness or good standing, none of which the register records.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
