How to read the ranking

Follow observed means a direct external link without nofollow, ugc or sponsored was inspected on a public listing. Conditional / mixed means templates or tiers differed. Follow claim to verify is a discovery lead, not verified backlink evidence.

The opportunity score weights evidence first, then availability, a visible submission route, platform relevance, price, Ahrefs Domain Rating and listing indexability. Paid placements should use the appropriate link qualification; this table does not promise ranking gains.
