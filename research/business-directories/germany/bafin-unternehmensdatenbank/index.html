Company database

The BaFin company database is the German financial supervisor’s searchable record of the undertakings it supervises. It covers credit institutions, leasing and factoring institutions, financial services institutions, capital management companies and further supervised categories, and carries the date its data was last updated.

Assessment

Strengths

  • It is the supervisor’s own database, so it is the national source rather than a European compilation of national notifications.
  • It distinguishes supervised categories explicitly, so a credit institution is not conflated with a financial services institution or a capital management company.
  • The page states the date to which its data is current, which makes the freshness of an answer checkable rather than assumed.

Limitations

  • It records supervision status only. It is not a statement about an institution’s solvency, conduct or the suitability of its products.
  • Warning lists and unauthorised-business notices are published elsewhere by the same supervisor, so this database is not where a reader learns that a firm is not permitted.
  • No query was executed during verification, so result formatting, export options and any rate limit are unestablished and are not asserted.

Best for

  • Checking whether a German financial undertaking is supervised, and in which supervised category it falls.
  • Reaching the supervisor’s own record of an institution rather than an aggregated European compilation.

Not recommended for

  • Finding warnings about unauthorised firms, which the supervisor publishes separately from this database.
  • Treating supervision as endorsement. The database records that an undertaking is supervised, not that it is sound or well run.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
