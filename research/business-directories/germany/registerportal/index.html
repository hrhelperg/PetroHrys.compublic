Gemeinsames Registerportal der Länder

Gemeinsames Registerportal der Länder is the official electronic system through which the German commercial, cooperative, partnership, company and association registers are searched and their filed documents inspected. The registers themselves are kept by the competent local courts; the portal is the retrieval route the Länder justice administrations designated for them, and it also carries the register announcements.

Assessment

Strengths

  • One portal reaches the registers of every Bundesland, which are otherwise kept court by court.
  • It covers the commercial, cooperative, partnership, company and association registers, and the register announcements alongside them.
  • Documents filed with the register can be inspected through it, not only the register entries themselves.
  • A court can certify that data retrieved through the portal matches the register content, so an official copy is obtainable by this route.

Limitations

  • The portal is the designated retrieval system, not the register itself. Under the Commercial Code the commercial register is kept electronically by the courts, and the portal is the electronic information and communication system the Länder justice administrations determined for access to it.
  • The operator describes structured retrieval as a non-binding service whose content may differ from the current official printout.
  • Because each register is kept by a local court, a question about the legal status of an entry belongs to that court rather than to the portal.

Best for

  • Searching any German register entry from a single national entry point rather than court by court.
  • Inspecting the documents an entity has filed with its commercial register.

Not recommended for

  • Treating a retrieval as the register itself, since the registers are kept by the courts and the portal provides access to them.
  • Relying on structured retrieval where the official printout is what is needed, because the operator describes the structured service as non-binding.
