Who Supplies What

A German-language B2B supplier marketplace covering the DACH region. Suppliers create a company profile and upload product data to be found by professional buyers, which makes it a procurement discovery platform rather than a local business directory.

Assessment

Strengths

  • The population is stated plainly: the platform describes itself as covering “600.000 B2B-Anbieter” and as “der meistbesuchte B2B-Marktplatz für den professionellen Einkauf in der DACH-Region”.
  • Profiles carry product data, not just contact details, which is what a procurement search actually needs.
  • Its reach is regional rather than national — Germany, Austria and Switzerland — which suits suppliers who already sell across DACH.

Limitations

  • Whether a basic profile is free is NOT documented. A premium profile is offered on request, but the existence of a paid tier does not establish that the basic tier is free, so cost is recorded as unknown.
  • It is a B2B marketplace. Presence here says nothing about local or consumer visibility, and it is the wrong platform for a business whose customers are the public.
  • Supplier badges indicate that some verification exists, but the operator does not document what is required or how it is checked, so nothing about verification is asserted.
  • No review system is documented, so neither reviews nor owner responses are asserted.
  • Coverage is described as the DACH region rather than Germany alone, so a record filed under Germany understates its geography. The dataset does not yet carry a machine-readable coverage field, and this is stated in prose instead.

Best for

  • German-speaking B2B suppliers wanting to be found by professional buyers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
  • Manufacturers and wholesalers whose discovery problem is procurement rather than local footfall.

Not recommended for

  • Local consumer-facing businesses. This is a B2B procurement marketplace, not a local directory.
  • Anyone assuming a basic profile is free. The cost of a basic profile is NOT documented and is recorded as unknown.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
