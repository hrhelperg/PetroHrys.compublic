Official Nationwide Register of Lawyers

The official nationwide directory of lawyers admitted in Germany, operated by the Bundesrechtsanwaltskammer over the registers of the regional bars. It answers one question — whether a person is admitted to practise as a Rechtsanwalt in Germany — and admission is required to practise, not merely to use the title.

Assessment

Strengths

  • It is the official nationwide directory and covers every lawyer admitted in Germany, so one search replaces twenty-eight regional ones.
  • It reaches beyond German lawyers to European lawyers and to lawyers from other states established in Germany under section 206 of the Federal Lawyers Act.
  • It is served in both German and English, which is unusual for a national professional register.

Limitations

  • Admission is required to practise, not only to use the title, so an entry means more here than in a protected-title register — but it still says nothing about competence in any field.
  • The federal chamber operates the directory; it is not the body that admitted anyone. Admission and discipline sit with the regional bar, which the search page states directly.
  • It records individual lawyers and their firms, not a firm’s quality, and specialist subtitles are conferred by the local bar rather than centrally.
  • The chamber states it is not entitled to give legal advice, to examine the conduct of individual lawyers, to recommend them, or to check fee invoices, so inclusion establishes none of those things.
  • No search was executed during verification, so result content and any limits were not observed and none is asserted.

Best for

  • Checking whether a named person is admitted to practise as a lawyer anywhere in Germany.
  • Confirming that a European or non-German lawyer is established in Germany under the Federal Lawyers Act.

Not recommended for

  • Judging whether a lawyer is suitable for a matter. The chamber states it may not assess or recommend individual lawyers.
  • Treating a nil result as proof that someone never practised. Admission can end, and the directory shows current status.
