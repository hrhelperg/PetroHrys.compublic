How these were selected

Nothing on this page is a curated list. Every actionable platform in the database was scored against one declaration of what AI startups need, and the ranking is what came back.

  • Fit comes first. Where a platform states which business types it accepts, that statement is used. Where it does not, the platform's category is used, and failing that the words in its own description. Each row shows which of the three applied.
  • A platform that states it does NOT accept this kind of business is excluded outright, however good it is.
  • Quality is the Directory Score where there is enough evidence to compute one, and a discounted fallback from reputation and status where there is not.

Limitations

These rankings reflect evidence recorded, not a survey of outcomes. A well-documented platform can outrank a better one that has been researched less. Rows marked Possible or Marginal rest on a category or a keyword rather than the platform's own statement of who it accepts — treat them as candidates to check, not conclusions.
