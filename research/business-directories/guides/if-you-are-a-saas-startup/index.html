If You Are a SaaS Startup

A directory plan for an early-stage SaaS company, drawn only from the verified dataset.

Introduction

An early SaaS company has limited attention and no review corpus yet. That combination rules out most of what is usually recommended: a comparison directory profile with no reviews on it is a weak signal, not a neutral one.

The sequence below is designed around that constraint. It front-loads the listings that work without social proof and defers the ones that need it.

Evidence base

Evidence base: 7 of 295 verified directories in the PetroHrys dataset accept both SaaS products and startups. Every figure and table on this page is generated from those records at build time. Fields that were never established are shown as Unknown rather than filled in.

Decision framework

  • Confirm your statutory register entry, which buyers will check during procurement.
  • List on any platform marketplace you integrate with. Distribution into an existing user base does not depend on your review count.
  • Choose one review platform and build a review process before creating the profile.
  • Defer the remaining comparison directories until you can populate them credibly.

Advantages recorded across these directories

  • The Canadian Trademarks Database can be searched free of charge and without an account, directly from the Canadian Intellectual Property Office.
  • CIPO states the database "provides access to over 140 years of Canadian trademarks data" and that a searcher can "search and study more than 1.4 million Canadian trademarks".
  • Coverage includes "all active and inactive marks that were cancelled, expunged, abandoned or refused after 1979", so a lapsed mark stays traceable.
  • The database publishes its own currency on the search page, which read 2026-08-05 when this record was verified.
  • Free to search, no account required
  • Authoritative federal record

Disadvantages recorded across these directories

  • It records trademarks, not businesses. An entry establishes nothing about whether the owner is an incorporated or registered entity, and nothing about its legitimacy or standing.
  • Unregistered common-law trademark rights exist in Canada and appear nowhere in the database, so a clear search is not a clearance opinion.
  • CIPO publishes a disclaimer about relying on the database and advises that searching intellectual property is complex enough to warrant a registered agent.
  • The Domain Rating shown is a dated measurement of the shared departmental domain ised-isde.canada.ca, not of this database.
  • Covers marks rather than companies
  • CIPO states that patent applications filed less than 18 months ago are only available if the applicant specifically asks for them, so recent filings can be invisible.

Recommendations

Work through the framework above in order and stop at the point where the next listing has no specific purpose.

When not to use this

  • Companies with no product in market yet. A listing precedes nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Should we launch on a launch platform first?

Only when the product is ready for scrutiny. A launch is a single-day event that rewards preparation and is largely wasted on an unfinished product.
