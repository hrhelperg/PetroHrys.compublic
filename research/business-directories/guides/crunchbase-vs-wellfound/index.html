Crunchbase vs Wellfound

A field-by-field comparison of Crunchbase and Wellfound using only verified data, including where the evidence is insufficient.

Introduction

Both profile startups, but they are read by different people for different reasons. One is consulted during diligence and market mapping; the other is consulted by candidates deciding where to work.

Advantages: Crunchbase

  • Widely cited funding and investor dataset
  • Free profile creation route exists

Advantages: Wellfound

  • Company profiles and job posting are free
  • Concentrated startup audience

Disadvantages: Crunchbase

  • Most analytical depth sits behind a paid plan
  • No public review system

Disadvantages: Wellfound

  • Oriented to hiring rather than general company discovery

Which to choose

Neither is a substitute for the other. Read the table above against your own situation: the fields that differ are the ones that should decide it, and the fields marked Unknown are questions to put to the directory rather than assumptions to make.

Where the two scores differ, the gap reflects the ten editorial factors documented in the scoring methodology, not popularity or traffic.

Frequently asked questions

Is one of these better than the other?

They are not interchangeable, so the question is which fits a given situation. This comparison sets out the verified differences rather than declaring a winner.

Why are some fields Unknown?

Because they were not stated on the official sources that were verified. They are shown as Unknown rather than estimated.
