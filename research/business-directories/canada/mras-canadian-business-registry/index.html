MRAS Canadian Business Registry

MRAS Canadian Business Registry is a federal search interface that queries participating provincial, territorial and federal corporate registers through the Multi-Jurisdictional Registry Access Service. It is a cross-registry lookup layer operated by Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada, not itself a register of record.

Assessment

Strengths

  • It is operated by the Government of Canada rather than by a commercial aggregator, and canadasbusinessregistries.ca resolves to ised-isde.canada.ca.
  • It gives one federal entry point to corporate records that are otherwise split across separate provincial and territorial systems.

Limitations

  • It is a search interface over participating registers, not the legal source of record for any entity.
  • Coverage is limited to the jurisdictions that participate in the Multi-Jurisdictional Registry Access Service, so a Canadian corporation may be absent without being unregistered.
  • The interface renders through client-side scripts, so its search and access behaviour could not be established from the served page alone.

Best for

  • Locating which Canadian registry holds a corporation when the jurisdiction of incorporation is not known.
  • Moving from a federal starting point to the participating provincial register that actually holds the file.

Not recommended for

  • Use as the legal source of record, which remains the federal, provincial or territorial register the entity is filed in.
  • Establishing coverage across Canada, because only some provinces and territories participate.
