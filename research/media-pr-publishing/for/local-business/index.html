How these were selected

This profile is declared abstractly: publication categories (local-business-media), industries (general) and subject keywords. It names no platform. Every result below is produced by matching those declarations against the registry, so the same rules that ranked this page rank every other one.

A recommendation combines four things: how well the publication serves this kind of business, whether its opportunity type delivers the objective, whether it reaches the target market, and the intrinsic Media Score of the opportunity. A platform with no Media Score is still recommended, at a discount, because an unscored platform reflects a gap in our research rather than a fault in the platform.

Explicit negative evidence disqualifies: a platform that has closed, was rejected on quality grounds, or offers no opportunity type capable of delivering the objective is excluded outright rather than ranked low. Missing evidence never excludes.

Limitations

The ranking is only as good as the underlying facts. Where a platform sits behind a bot filter its route could not be read, and the row says so — treat those as leads to confirm in a browser, not as verified opportunities.

Media Score carries no traffic, Domain Rating or audience figures, because this repository measures none of those and will not estimate them. It is an editorial reading of the opportunity, not a prediction of results.
