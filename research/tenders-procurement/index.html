Methodology

Every platform was verified against its own live surface: HTTP status, final URL, page title and body size were recorded, and a deep route is published only when it was separately confirmed to differ from the homepage. A 200 response is not treated as proof, and a bot wall or a script-only shell is never treated as a dead platform — those records say "Needs browser check" instead.

Statements with consequences carry the highest evidence bar. That a foreign supplier may participate is claimed only where the operator or a competent authority states it; it is never inferred from a website being reachable, being in English, or belonging to an EU member state.

This database catalogues platforms. It does not republish tender notices, and it records no invented metrics — no traffic estimates, no contract values, no bidder counts, no scores.

What this database is — and is not

A procurement platform here is a system where a company can discover tenders or contract notices, register as a supplier, obtain tender documents, or submit a bid. National systems, EU and institutional systems, development banks and genuinely distinct ecosystem components each get one record.

It is not a grants database, a business directory, a marketplace, a list of procurement authorities, or a list of procurement software vendors. EU funding portals are procurement look-alikes and are deliberately excluded; a regulator that merely supervises a platform is recorded as the operator of that platform, not as a platform itself.

Limitations

Some official platforms sit behind bot protection, cookie gates or script-only shells that cannot be verified without a rendered browser. Those records are published with "Needs browser check" and without any route this research could not confirm.

Coverage is Wave T1: Europe and adjacent jurisdictions, prioritising official national systems and EU-level infrastructure. Regional, municipal, utility and commercial-aggregator systems are researched in later waves. Russia and Belarus are recorded as unresolved pending a sanctions and accessibility assessment.
