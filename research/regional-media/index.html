How to read this registry

World region, subregion, country and coverage area describe the audience geography; they do not imply that an outlet accepts submissions.

Domain Rating is an Ahrefs snapshot of domain-level backlink strength, not a quality score or a guarantee that a future article will rank.

Follow, restricted and indexability states require evidence from a named public page. Unknown means no page-level reading has been recorded yet.
