The Local

One of Germany’s three long-established telephone and business directories. Its entry service is run by DTM Deutsche Tele Medien and is notable for what it does openly: a single free submission places a business in Das Örtliche, Das Telefonbuch and Gelbe Seiten at once.

Assessment

Strengths

  • The entry is free and the operator states it without qualification: “Ihr Eintrag. Mit nur 1 Klick direkt in 3 Verzeichnissen. Kostenfrei.”
  • One submission reaches three of Germany’s best-known directories: “Freuen Sie sich auf Ihren Eintrag in Das Telefonbuch, Gelbe Seiten und Das Örtliche.”
  • The operator is named plainly as DTM Deutsche Tele Medien GmbH, with separate documented routes for creating, editing and deleting an entry.
  • Both new entries and changes to an existing one are handled through the same service: “Hier können Sie sich oder Ihre Firma neu eintragen oder Ihren bestehenden Eintrag verwalten.”

Limitations

  • A submission is not confined to this directory. The same form publishes to Das Telefonbuch and Gelbe Seiten, which is convenient but removes the choice of appearing in only one.
  • The directory itself is a separate surface from the entry service: dasoertliche.de carries the listings, dtme.de takes the submissions. A business must leave the directory to manage its own data.
  • Creating and updating an entry are documented; taking control of an existing third-party entry as its owner is not described as a claim process, so no claim flow is asserted.
  • Whether verification is required, and by what method, is not documented, so nothing about verification is asserted.
  • No review system or owner-response function is documented on the pages consulted, so neither is asserted. Absence here means unrecorded, not unavailable.
  • No Domain Rating is published for this platform. No valid measurement exists in the frozen historical set and none may be estimated, so the metric is recorded as not measured rather than guessed.
  • A listing is a directory entry. It is not evidence that a business is solvent, currently trading, licensed, or checked beyond what the operator documents here.
  • No ranking, indexing, traffic or lead outcome is asserted. The operator markets outcomes; this record does not adopt them.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
