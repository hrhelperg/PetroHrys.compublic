Canadian Trademarks Database

Canadian Trademarks Database is the federal trademark register published by the Canadian Intellectual Property Office. It carries applied-for and registered Canadian trademarks together with certification marks, distinguishing guises, geographical indications and plant breeders’ rights denominations, searchable by mark, owner, application or registration number and international registration number.

Einschätzung

Stärken

  • The Canadian Trademarks Database can be searched free of charge and without an account, directly from the Canadian Intellectual Property Office.
  • CIPO states the database "provides access to over 140 years of Canadian trademarks data" and that a searcher can "search and study more than 1.4 million Canadian trademarks".
  • Coverage includes "all active and inactive marks that were cancelled, expunged, abandoned or refused after 1979", so a lapsed mark stays traceable.
  • The database publishes its own currency on the search page, which read 2026-08-05 when this record was verified.

Einschränkungen

  • It records trademarks, not businesses. An entry establishes nothing about whether the owner is an incorporated or registered entity, and nothing about its legitimacy or standing.
  • Unregistered common-law trademark rights exist in Canada and appear nowhere in the database, so a clear search is not a clearance opinion.
  • CIPO publishes a disclaimer about relying on the database and advises that searching intellectual property is complex enough to warrant a registered agent.
  • The Domain Rating shown is a dated measurement of the shared departmental domain ised-isde.canada.ca, not of this database.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
