Herold

Einschätzung

Stärken

  • The entry is free and the operator says so in the page’s own heading: “Kostenlosen Firmeneintrag auf Herold erstellen”.
  • Both routes are named by the operator, which is why this is recorded as create-and-claim: “Eintrag hinzufügen” and “Eintrag beanspruchen”, alongside “Eintrag verwalten” for managing it afterwards.
  • The operator describes the platform as “Österreichs größtem Branchen- und Firmenverzeichnis”, and identifies itself in the footer as HEROLD Business Data GmbH.
  • One form handles creating and claiming, so a business that is unsure whether it is already listed does not have to guess.

Einschränkungen

  • The operator markets “Besseres Google-Ranking” as a benefit. That is a marketing claim and is NOT adopted here; no ranking outcome is asserted.
  • Whether verification is required, and by what method, is not documented, so nothing about verification is asserted.
  • No review system or owner-response function is documented on the pages consulted, so neither is asserted. Absence here means unrecorded, not unavailable.
  • The free entry sits alongside paid promotion products, so the operator has a commercial interest in upselling after submission.
  • No Domain Rating is published for this platform. No valid measurement exists in the frozen historical set and none may be estimated, so the metric is recorded as not measured rather than guessed.
  • A listing is a directory entry. It is not evidence that a business is solvent, currently trading, licensed, or checked beyond what the operator documents here.
  • No ranking, indexing, traffic or lead outcome is asserted. The operator markets outcomes; this record does not adopt them.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
