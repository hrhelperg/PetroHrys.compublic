Scottish Charity Register

The Scottish Charity Register is the statutory register of Scottish charities kept by the Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator. It records each charity’s registered number, purposes, principal contact, accounts and the local authority areas in which it operates.

Einschätzung

Stärken

  • The Scottish Charity Register can be searched free of charge and without an account.
  • It is kept by a non-ministerial office independent of the Scottish Government that reports directly to the Scottish Parliament.
  • Results can be filtered by Scottish local authority area, which makes locating a local charity straightforward.

Einschränkungen

  • It covers Scotland only. A charity operating across the United Kingdom may need to appear on two or three separate registers.
  • Registration establishes charitable status under Scottish charity law, not good governance, solvency or current activity.
  • Much of the published detail is self-reported by the charity, so it records what was declared rather than what was audited.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
