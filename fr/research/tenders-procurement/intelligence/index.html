Intelligence des marchés : 369 plateformes évaluées sur 7 profils de fournisseur

Quels systèmes de marchés étudier en premier selon votre type de fournisseur et votre objectif, avec le niveau de preuve et la raison de chaque recommandation.

Comment cela fonctionne

Two different questions are scored separately. Utility asks how strong and actionable a procurement source is: can opportunities be found, is there a registration path, can a bid be lodged, and how well established are those facts. Fit asks how relevant that source is to a particular supplier and objective. A national platform with modest evidence can be the single most relevant source for a supplier targeting that country, and a superbly documented development bank can be irrelevant to a local firm.

Every value here is derived at build time from the platform records. Nothing is stored, nothing is estimated, and no platform is promoted by hand. Where a fact was never established, the answer is unknown rather than a guess.

The database records no industry classification. Recommendations for software, telecom, manufacturing or construction suppliers rank general-purpose systems by the capabilities those suppliers need — they do not claim any platform specialises in a sector.

Recommandations par type de fournisseur

Ranked from 383 publishable platforms. Fit orders each list; utility breaks ties.

Foreign supplier eligibility is verified on 37 platforms and unverified on 345. Unverified means exactly that — it is not a finding that foreign suppliers are excluded.

195 platforms still require verification in a rendered browser. That is a research state, not a quality judgement: a system behind bot protection is not a worse procurement system.

Limites

Scores measure documented procurement utility, not popularity, contract volume or likelihood of winning. None of those is recorded and none is estimated.

Industry recommendations are an editorial mapping from supplier type to the capabilities that matter, applied over recorded facts. They are labelled as editorial wherever they appear.

14 platforms are not yet scored because their own evidence is not established. Scoring them would present a research gap as a measurement.
