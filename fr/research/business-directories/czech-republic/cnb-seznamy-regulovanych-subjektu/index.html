Lists of regulated and registered financial market entities

The Czech National Bank publishes a single application over its lists of regulated and registered financial market entities. It offers direct search, the underlying basic lists, and entity counts and time series over the same data.

Évaluation

Points forts

  • It is the central bank’s own application, covering regulated and registered populations together in one system.
  • Direct search, the underlying lists and statistical views sit in one place, so a user can move between an individual check and the population.
  • The central bank also publishes web services alongside it, so the same data is reachable programmatically.

Limites

  • It distinguishes regulated from merely registered entities, and the two carry different regulatory weight; a hit alone does not tell a reader which applies.
  • No query was executed during verification, so result content and any limits are unestablished and none is asserted.
  • A financial supervisory list does not establish that an entity legally exists, which is a matter for the public register kept by the courts.
  • The published address is the bank’s own entry point and redirects to a separate application host, so a bookmark or a citation may not land on the address a reader started from.

Idéal pour

  • Checking whether a financial market entity is regulated or registered in Czechia, and in which population.
  • Reading the central bank’s own lists rather than a secondary reproduction of them.

Non recommandé pour

  • Company verification, which is the public register kept by the courts rather than a financial supervisory list.
  • Treating registration as endorsement. Being regulated or registered is not a statement about solvency or conduct.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
