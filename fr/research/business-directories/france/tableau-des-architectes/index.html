Roll of the Order of Architects

The roll of the French Order of Architects, kept and continuously updated by the regional councils of the Order. Entry on the roll is required to use the title of architect and to carry out the design work reserved to the profession, and the roll distinguishes architects entitled to design work from those registered for other activities.

Traduction éditoriale

Évaluation

Points forts

  • It is the roll of the Order itself, kept continuously up to date by the regional councils rather than compiled periodically.
  • It separates architects entitled to design, interior and exterior layout, urban planning and works supervision from architects registered for other activities such as teaching, journalism or expert work.
  • It covers companies as well as individuals, and distinguishes liberal, salaried, associate, public-servant and company modes of practice.
  • It reaches the overseas regions, including Guadeloupe, Guyane, Martinique and Réunion-Mayotte, not only metropolitan France.

Limites

  • Entry is required both to use the title and to carry out reserved design work, so the two effects coincide here — but an entry is still not a statement about the quality of any project.
  • Architects registered for other activities are on the roll but are NOT entitled to design work and, as the roll states, are not insured for it. Reading any hit as a designer is an error.
  • The roll is maintained by the regional councils of the Order, so the national interface aggregates regional decisions and the Order directs doubtful cases back to the regional council.
  • Several adjacent lists exist beside the roll — holders of a récépissé, service providers, fee lists and a special list of architects’ holding companies — and a search of the roll does not cover them.
  • Absence from the roll does not establish that a person never practised, and it does not exhaust the Order’s lists. Entry can end, the adjacent lists are searched separately, and the Order directs doubtful cases to the regional council.
  • No search was executed during verification, so result content and any limits were not observed and none is asserted.

Idéal pour

  • Checking that a named architect or architectural company is on the roll of the Order.

Non recommandé pour

  • Choosing an architect for a project. A separate finder exists for that and is not this register.
  • Assuming every architect found is insured for design work. The roll states that some are not.
