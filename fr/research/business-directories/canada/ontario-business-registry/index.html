Ontario Business Registry

Ontario Business Registry is the provincial register of Ontario corporations, not-for-profit corporations and registered business names, operated by the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement. Basic entity information is searchable at no charge, while profile reports, certificates and document copies are priced products.

Assessment

Strengths

  • Ontario states that "Anyone can search the OBR for free to get basic information about a business or not-for-profit organization".
  • No account is needed to run a basic search, so a counterparty check costs nothing.
  • It is the single provincial register for Ontario corporations, not-for-profit corporations and registered business names.

Limitations

  • The free tier returns basic information only; a profile report is $8, a document copy is $3 and a certificate of status is $26.
  • It records Ontario registrations and does not establish federal incorporation.
  • Registrants must report changes within 15 days, so a record can lag a recent change by up to that period.

Submission guidance

Records are created through incorporation, registration, filing, or statutory processes.

Best for

  • Confirming that an Ontario corporation, not-for-profit or business name is on the provincial register.
  • Establishing basic entity status before deciding whether a priced profile report is worth ordering.

Not recommended for

  • Confirming federal incorporation, which is recorded by Corporations Canada rather than by Ontario.
  • Obtaining directors, officers or filing history at no cost, because those sit behind priced products.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
