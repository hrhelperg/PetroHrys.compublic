If You Are an Enterprise

A directory plan for an established enterprise vendor, drawn only from the verified dataset.

Introduction

An enterprise vendor's problem is not visibility but consistency. Listings already exist, often created years ago by different teams, and the risk is that a procurement reviewer finds three descriptions that disagree.

The work here is consolidation before expansion.

Evidence base

Evidence base: 39 of 295 verified directories in the PetroHrys dataset accept enterprise vendors and require verification. Every figure and table on this page is generated from those records at build time. Fields that were never established are shown as Unknown rather than filled in.

Decision framework

  • Inventory existing listings and claim any unclaimed profiles.
  • Standardise the description, categories and contact details across all of them.
  • Confirm the statutory register entry matches the legal entity used in contracts.
  • Only then consider new listings, prioritising directories that verify their reviewers.

Advantages recorded across these directories

  • Primary legal source for UK company data
  • Free public access to filings
  • Free, no login required
  • Authoritative ABN status and GST data
  • Free across every register
  • Includes banned and disqualified registers, which most registries omit

Disadvantages recorded across these directories

  • Companies House states it does not verify the accuracy of filed information
  • Limited to publicly disclosable ABR fields
  • Split across many separate register searches
  • It is a consultation interface, not the legal source of record. The registers it draws on — the Registre National des Entreprises and the Base Sirene among them — remain the authoritative sources, and a legal question belongs to them.
  • Because it presents data assembled from several administrations, a discrepancy is resolved against the originating register rather than against this interface.
  • The interface is French-language.

Recommendations

Work through the framework above in order and stop at the point where the next listing has no specific purpose.

When not to use this

  • Teams treating directories as a lead-generation channel in isolation.

Frequently asked questions

Are unclaimed profiles a risk?

They can be. A profile you do not control may carry stale information, and several directories allow reviews on unclaimed listings.
