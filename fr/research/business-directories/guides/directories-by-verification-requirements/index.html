Directories by Verification Requirements

Which verified directories require you to prove something before publishing, and what that implies for effort.

Introduction

Verification requirements are the clearest available proxy for how much effort a listing will take and how much a listing is worth. A directory that publishes anything anyone submits is cheap to appear in and correspondingly cheap as a signal. One that requires proof of identity, domain control or an established account is more work and means more.

This is one of the few effort-related fields this dataset can populate honestly, because it is usually stated on the directory's own pages. Approval time and review process are not, and remain null throughout.

Evidence base

Evidence base: 61 of 295 verified directories in the PetroHrys dataset require verification before listing. Every figure and table on this page is generated from those records at build time. Fields that were never established are shown as Unknown rather than filled in.

Decision framework

  • Budget effort by requirement, not by directory size. A small gated registry can take longer than a large open one.
  • Gather proof once. Domain control, company registration details and a consistent description cover most requirements.
  • Expect gated directories to reject inconsistencies between your listing and your public record.

Advantages recorded across these directories

  • Primary legal source for UK company data
  • Free public access to filings
  • Free, no login required
  • Authoritative ABN status and GST data
  • Free across every register
  • Includes banned and disqualified registers, which most registries omit

Disadvantages recorded across these directories

  • Companies House states it does not verify the accuracy of filed information
  • Limited to publicly disclosable ABR fields
  • Split across many separate register searches
  • It is a consultation interface, not the legal source of record. The registers it draws on — the Registre National des Entreprises and the Base Sirene among them — remain the authoritative sources, and a legal question belongs to them.
  • Because it presents data assembled from several administrations, a discrepancy is resolved against the originating register rather than against this interface.
  • The interface is French-language.

Recommendations

Treat a verification requirement as a positive signal about the directory rather than an obstacle. The directories that check are the ones whose listings a reader can trust.

When not to use this

  • Anyone looking for the fastest possible route to a large number of listings.

Frequently asked questions

Does verification required mean identity documents?

Not necessarily. It means the directory requires some form of confirmation before a listing is published, which may be an account, domain control, or company details. This dataset does not record which, so it is not stated.
