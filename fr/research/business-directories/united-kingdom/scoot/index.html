Scoot

A UK business directory operated by Newfold Digital. One submission does not produce one listing: the operator states that adding a business here automatically adds it to a wider network of directories including Touch Local, and that the submitted details are shared with suppliers who may market to the business.

Évaluation

Points forts

  • A free entry is documented by the operator: “We are easy to join and give significant value to small businesses for FREE”, alongside “a range of paid for services”.
  • The submission is a real, complete form — name, business name, category, postcode, address, email, phone and web address — described as “Step 1 of 1 - Business Details”.
  • The business can maintain its own entry afterwards: “Have the ability to update your profile 24/7 from your Scoot Dashboard”.
  • The operator discloses the onward use of submitted data before you submit it, rather than burying it in terms.

Limites

  • Your details will be used to market to you. The form states the information “will be used to contact you by users of these directories … and suppliers (including https://web.com/en-gb ) who may wish to provide services to businesses like yours”.
  • One submission is not one listing. The operator states: “By adding yourself to this directory you will be automatically added to our network of directories including Scoot and Touch Local.” Where else an entry appears across that network is not enumerated.
  • The operator describes a network “with over 400 websites”. That figure is the operator’s own claim and has not been independently checked.
  • Creating an entry is confirmed; claiming an existing third-party entry is not documented anywhere, so no claim flow is asserted.
  • Whether verification is required, and by what method, is not documented, so nothing about verification is asserted.
  • Listings carry reviews — public results invite a reader to “Be the first to review” — but whether a business can respond to them is not documented and is not asserted.
  • A listing is a directory entry. It is not evidence that a business is solvent, currently trading, licensed, or checked beyond what the operator documents here.
  • No ranking, indexing, traffic or lead outcome is asserted. The operator markets outcomes; this record does not adopt them.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
