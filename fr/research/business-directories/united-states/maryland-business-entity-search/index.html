Business Entity Search

Business Entity Search is the state business-entity register operated by the Maryland State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT). Search fields: Three search modes, verified on the live page: Business Name, Department ID, and Employer Identification Number (EIN). Whether filed document images can be viewed free of charge is not stated on the official pages.

Évaluation

Points forts

  • Business Entity Search can be searched free of charge and without an account, directly from the Maryland State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT).
  • The Maryland State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT) is the statutory source for Maryland, so an entry exists because a filing was made — not because anyone submitted a listing.

Limites

  • A CAPTCHA gates the Maryland search, which blocks automated and repeated lookups.
  • No official page states whether filed document images can be viewed free of charge, so the cost of reading a filing cannot be established in advance.
  • Part of Business Entity Search sits behind payment, an account or another restriction, so the free tier answers only part of a due-diligence question.
  • A Maryland Business Express search result evidences a charter or qualification record held by SDAT. It does not prove good standing — SDAT treats good standing as a separate enquiry with its own contact channel, and 'Active' in the search is not a good-standing certificate.

The PetroHrys Score is a first-party editorial assessment, not a third-party authority metric. It is the weighted sum of the ten factors above, divided by ten, so the number on this page is reproducible from the values shown. Each factor is scored from 0 to 10 by a human reviewer and weighted as shown. The definitions describe what was weighed, not a rubric: no threshold or formula maps a directory onto a value, and two reviewers could reach different numbers. The score is reproducible from the factors shown on every record, but it is an editorial judgement, not a measurement.
