eSIMky

Rent mobile data for one trip. Choose a country and a number of days, pay by card, and the eSIM QR code arrives by email in about a minute.

What an eSIM is

An eSIM is a mobile subscriber profile stored in the phone itself rather than on a removable card. Installing one means scanning a QR code in your phone settings — there is no card to collect, post or swap.

A phone that supports eSIM can hold your usual number and a travel data profile at the same time, so your number keeps working for calls and messages while data runs over the rented profile. This is a rental for one trip, not a SIM you buy and keep.

Open esimky.com →

How the rental works

  • ChoosePick the destination and the length of the trip. Plans are compared by price per gigabyte.→
  • ReserveCheckout is handled by Stripe, with the all-in price shown before payment.→
  • ReceiveThe QR code arrives by email, typically within about a minute. Install it before you travel.→
  • UseData runs for the period rented. When the trip ends the profile expires; there is no contract to cancel.→

What it does not do

It is not a phone number. The rental carries data only, and your existing SIM and number are untouched. If you need a number abroad as well, that is what TwinPhone does.

Your handset has to support eSIM and must not be carrier-locked. That is the one requirement worth checking before renting: most recent iPhone and Android flagship models support eSIM, and older or budget handsets often do not.

Who it suits

  • TourismA short trip where maps, translation and messaging should work on arrival.→
  • Business travelA trip of known length where the data cost is fixed in advance and easier to expense than a roaming bill.→
  • Digital nomadsMoving between countries and renting per destination rather than holding contracts in several.→
  • Remote work abroadWorking from another country and needing a connection independent of the accommodation Wi-Fi.→
  • International teamsColleagues travelling to the same event, each renting for the same dates, cost known up front.→
  • Students and frequent travellersA term abroad, or repeated short trips where installing a profile before departure is quicker than arranging connectivity on arrival.→

Questions

What exactly am I renting?

A temporary prepaid data plan on an eSIM profile, for a chosen country and number of days.

Is this a phone number?

No. Data only. Your existing number stays on your normal SIM.

When should I install it?

Before you travel, while you still have a connection. It activates for the destination rather than at the moment you scan it.

Do I need an app?

No, and there are none yet for iOS or Android.

Will my phone work with it?

Only if it supports eSIM and is not carrier-locked. Most recent iPhone and Android flagship models do; older and some budget handsets do not.
