eSIMky — rent mobile data for a trip

Choose a country and a number of days, pay by card, and the eSIM QR code arrives by email in about a minute. It is a rental for one trip, not a SIM you keep.

Open eSIMky How it works

What an eSIM is

A SIM without the plastic An eSIM is a mobile subscriber profile stored in the phone itself rather than on a removable card. Installing one means scanning a QR code in your phone settings — there is no card to collect, post or swap.
It sits alongside your normal SIM A phone that supports eSIM can hold your usual number and a travel data profile at the same time. Your number keeps working for calls and messages while the data runs over the rented profile.
It replaces roaming, not your number The rental provides data only. That is the point of difference from roaming, where the same account carries both and the bill arrives afterwards.

How the rental works

1

Choose country and days

Pick the destination and the length of the trip. Plans are compared by price per gigabyte, so the cheaper option for your usage is visible before you pay.

2

Reserve the rental

Checkout is handled by Stripe. The all-in price is shown before payment rather than assembled from add-ons afterwards.

3

Get the QR by email

The QR code arrives by email, typically within about a minute. Install it before you travel and it activates on arrival.

4

Use it for the trip

Data runs for the period you rented. When the trip ends the profile simply expires; there is no contract to cancel.

What it does and does not do

📶

Data for the destination

Prepaid data on a local or regional network for the country you selected, for the number of days you selected.

✉️

Delivered by email

The QR code is emailed to you. There is no app to install and no account software to keep on the phone.

🔢

Your number is untouched

This is not a phone number and does not replace one. Your existing SIM and number stay exactly as they are.

💳

Price shown before you pay

Plans are listed with an all-in price and a price per gigabyte, so two plans can be compared on the same basis.

🔒

Stripe checkout

Payment is processed by Stripe rather than handled on the site itself.

📱

Requires an eSIM-capable phone

The handset must support eSIM and must not be carrier-locked. This is the one requirement worth checking before renting.

Who it suits

Tourism

A short trip where you want maps, translation and messaging to work on arrival without hunting for a local SIM shop at the airport.

Business travel

A trip of known length where the data cost is fixed in advance, which is easier to expense than a roaming bill that arrives weeks later.

Digital nomads

Moving between countries every few weeks and renting data per destination rather than holding contracts in several of them.

Remote work abroad

Working from another country for a defined period and needing a connection that does not depend on the accommodation's Wi-Fi.

International teams

Colleagues travelling to the same event or office, each renting data for the same dates, with the cost known up front.

Students

An exchange term or a summer abroad, where a short rental is simpler than opening and then closing a local account.

Frequent travellers

Repeated short trips where installing a profile before departure is quicker than arranging connectivity at each destination.

Mobile apps

eSIMky works from the browser and needs no app — the QR arrives by email and is scanned in your phone settings. Mobile apps are not released yet.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly am I renting?+
A temporary prepaid data plan on an eSIM profile, for a chosen country and number of days. It is a rental for one trip rather than a SIM you keep afterwards.
Is this a phone number?+
No. The rental is data only. Your existing number stays on your normal SIM, so calls and messages to it keep working. If you need a number abroad as well, that is what Twin Phone does.
When should I install it?+
Install the profile before you travel, while you still have a connection. It activates for the destination rather than at the moment you scan it.
Will my phone work with it?+
The handset has to support eSIM and must not be carrier-locked. Most recent iPhone and Android flagship models do; older and some budget handsets do not.
Do I need an app?+
No. The QR code arrives by email and is scanned in your phone settings. Apps for iOS and Android are not available yet.
