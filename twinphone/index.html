TwinPhone

A phone line that runs in a browser. Call 145+ countries from $0.02 a minute, rent a virtual number, and give a whole team one shared business line.

What it is

TwinPhone places calls to real phone numbers from a browser tab. There is no SIM to buy, no handset to configure and no contract, and calls are encrypted in transit. You top up a balance and pay for what you use.

It exists because the alternatives are awkward: international roaming is billed after the fact at several dollars a minute, and international add-ons are tiered in ways that make the cost of a single call hard to predict.

Twin Phone Enterprise

A shared cloud phone for a whole team: one business number the company answers, rather than a line tied to one person's handset. Calls run over Twilio and Telnyx carrier networks.

Numbers on the enterprise plan cover the US and Canada — bring an existing number across or take a new one. Every call is recorded and stored against the contact, so what was said is available afterwards rather than a note about it. Call volume and spend break down by team member, and contacts and history are shared, so a colleague picking up a returning caller can see the previous conversation.

There is no subscription and no per-person fee. Adding a colleague does not add a licence cost, which is the main difference from seat-priced business telephony.

Open enterprise.twin-phone.com →

How businesses use it

  • Outbound salesA team dialling from one shared line, with each call logged against the contact and spend visible per representative.→
  • Customer supportWhoever is free answers the company number, and shared history means a returning caller does not repeat themselves.→
  • Call centres and BPOPer-person analytics and recorded calls give a quality-assurance trail without separate recording software.→
  • Distributed teamsColleagues in different countries answer the same line from a browser. No hardware to ship, no office PBX.→
  • International salesCalls to 145+ countries at per-minute rates, which is where the saving against conventional telephony sits.→
  • Real estate, e-commerce, clinicsAppointment and enquiry-driven businesses where several staff answer one published number and a missed call is a lost booking.→

Mobile apps

TwinPhone runs in a browser and needs no app. An iOS app is also available. An Android app has not been released yet.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a browser, so there is no SIM, no desk phone and no PBX. The iOS app is optional.

Which countries can I get a number in?

Calling covers 145+ countries. Numbers on the enterprise plan cover the US and Canada.

Is there a per-seat fee?

No. Enterprise has no subscription and no per-person fee.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet.
